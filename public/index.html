How to play

  1. Study. 20 seconds with the target article.
  2. Drop. Everyone starts on the same random page.
  3. Click. Links only. No search, no typing URLs.
  4. Compare. Fewer clicks and less time = higher score.

Party mode

Make a room, send the link, and race your friends on the same random pair. First one there takes Elo from everyone else.
